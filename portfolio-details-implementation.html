EMR/HIS Implementation Experience

I have more than five years of experience supporting healthcare IT implementation, with a focus on helping healthcare users adopt EMR/HIS applications and align system workflows with hospital operations.

My work includes coordinating with hospital stakeholders and technical teams, clarifying operational requirements, supporting configuration and data validation, assisting with User Acceptance Testing (UAT), delivering user guidance, and following up on issues during implementation.

Hospital Workflow & Requirements

Coordinate with users to understand operational processes, clarify requirements, and identify workflow needs across relevant hospital services. Help translate user feedback into actionable items for configuration, testing, or technical follow-up.

Configuration & Data Validation

Support application configuration and master data validation based on project requirements. Review data consistency, verify expected system behavior, and coordinate discrepancies with the relevant stakeholders or technical team.

UAT, Testing & User Training

Assist users during UAT by reviewing scenarios, recording findings, and coordinating issue follow-up. Provide system guidance and training so users can understand the agreed workflows before operational use.

Deployment & Post-Go-Live Support

Support go-live preparation and deployment coordination, help users report and clarify issues, and follow up with the relevant teams after release to support system adoption and continuity of hospital operations.

Implementation Approach

My approach is collaborative and workflow-oriented: understand user needs, confirm the expected process, support configuration and data checks, prepare and assist users during testing, coordinate deployment readiness, and follow up on issues after go-live. Activities and responsibilities are adjusted to the scope and needs of each implementation project.

Key Strengths

  • Healthcare application implementation and user coordination
  • Hospital workflow understanding and requirements clarification
  • UAT support, issue documentation, and follow-up coordination
  • User training, deployment readiness, and post-go-live support
