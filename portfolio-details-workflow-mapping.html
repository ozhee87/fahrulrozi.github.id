  1. Home

Project Information

  • Domain: Healthcare IT
  • Focus: Workflow Analysis & Master Data Mapping
  • Activities: Requirements Review, Mapping, Validation
  • Related Systems: EMR / HIS / SIMRS

Project Objective

Support healthcare system implementation by reviewing hospital workflows, organizing master data mapping, and validating data consistency to help align operational needs with system configuration.

This page demonstrates a generalized approach using fictional sample data and illustrative workflow documentation.

Hospital Workflow Diagram

This high-level example shows how an outpatient visit may move through registration, clinical assessment, optional diagnostic services, pharmacy or billing activities, and visit completion.

Illustrative only. Actual sequence depends on the hospital's policies, clinical needs, and EMR/HIS configuration.

Hospital Workflow Analysis

Review operational processes and clarify how activities are represented in the hospital information system.

  • Document relevant registration and service workflows.
  • Identify dependencies between service units.
  • Clarify operational requirements with stakeholders.

Master Data Mapping

Compare source reference values with target system fields according to agreed business rules and available master data references.

  • Review source and target fields.
  • Map relevant service, unit, or reference codes.
  • Flag unmatched, duplicate, or inconsistent values.

Data Validation & Issue Follow-up

Check mapping completeness and consistency, record exceptions, and coordinate clarification or resolution with relevant users and technical teams.

Implementation Support

Maintain mapping documentation, communicate findings to stakeholders, and support testing or implementation activities where applicable.

Approach

The approach combines workflow review, consistent mapping rules, documented validation, and stakeholder collaboration. Specific activities depend on the scope and requirements of each implementation.

Expected Value

  • Clearer understanding of operational workflows.
  • More consistent and traceable data mapping.
  • Earlier identification of discrepancies.
  • Clearer communication between business users and technical teams.
